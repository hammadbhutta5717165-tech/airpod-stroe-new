NEED TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

How do I place an order?

Choose a product and tap “Order on WhatsApp”. The store's WhatsApp chat opens with a draft message. Review it and press Send.

Are all products genuine Apple products?

Check the label and description on each product. Only call an item genuine if you have verified its authenticity. Compatible third-party products must be clearly labelled as compatible.

Can I pay cash on delivery?

Ask the store to confirm whether cash on delivery is available in your city, along with delivery charges and expected delivery time.

Are the prices final?

The included prices are sample values. The store owner should update them in script.js before publishing.
